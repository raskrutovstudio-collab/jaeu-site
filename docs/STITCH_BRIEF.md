# Google Stitch brief — JAEU home page

## Goal

Create a polished desktop and mobile homepage concept for the Association of Jewellers of the Eurasian Economic Union. This is an industry association and public-interest organisation, not an online jewellery store.

## Brand character

- credible;
- diplomatic;
- modern Eurasian institution;
- expert and legally precise;
- open to business dialogue;
- premium through typography and composition, not decoration.

## Visual system

- canvas: warm off-white and white;
- primary: deep emerald `#103E36`;
- secondary: dark graphite `#18201F`;
- text: graphite `#262D2B`;
- accent: restrained cool teal `#4F8075` and silver-grey `#B8C2BF`;
- borders: cool grey-green with low contrast;
- generous whitespace and editorial rhythm;
- elegant serif for selected display headings, neutral sans-serif for UI and body copy;
- real event photography and documentary imagery;
- use the existing logo, but give it enough clear space.

Avoid yellow and gold accents, black luxury-store styling, jewellery-shop catalog patterns, glossy product renders, purple gradients, generic card mosaics and decorative section numbers.

## Required desktop frame

- 1440 px wide;
- content container around 1240–1280 px;
- sticky but compact header;
- Russian navigation with visible RU / KZ / EN switch;
- primary action: “Вступить в Ассоциацию”;
- secondary action: “Направить обращение”.

## Homepage sections

1. Header with logo, navigation, language switch and membership action.
2. Hero: association name, clear mission, two actions and a strong documentary visual.
3. Trust strip: operating since 2016, EAEU scope and industry representation. Do not invent numeric metrics.
4. About: concise positioning and link to full profile.
5. Activity directions: regulatory dialogue, legal expertise, common market, standards, analytics and international cooperation.
6. Current priorities or industry agenda.
7. Expert Advisory Council feature.
8. Latest verified news and events using real photography.
9. Membership value and transparent joining path.
10. Members and partners area without invented logos.
11. Documents and knowledge entry point.
12. Contact / appeal block.
13. Full footer with legal identity, contacts, navigation and language links.

## UX requirements

- real responsive redesign at 390 px, not a compressed desktop;
- readable Russian text and realistic line lengths;
- no tiny labels or low-contrast gold body text;
- clear hover/focus states;
- no carousel for essential content;
- no text embedded inside images;
- sections must translate cleanly to Kazakh and English, allowing text expansion.

## Output

Generate two coherent screens from the same system:

1. desktop homepage at 1440 px;
2. mobile homepage at 390 px.

Use realistic Russian copy placeholders derived from this brief. Mark any unsupported facts as placeholders rather than presenting them as confirmed.
